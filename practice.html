Practice

There are many things that can prevent us from stargazing: light pollution, a cloudy night, or no telescope. That shouldn’t mean we can’t stargaze at all. Being introduced to Stellarium during an astronomy course in college made me realize that space exploration is for all of us.

Stellarium is a free stargazing software that allows you to explore the sky from any point on Earth. You can search for any celestial body and look at all of the information regarding it. These practice lessons are meant to help you apply what you learned in the mini topic lessons and guide you through digital stargazing.

You can download Stellarium here.

Notes before getting started:

You can check off items on the checklist on the website, or opt to print them out. Your findings can be recorded in any format of your choosing.


Under the Sky and Viewing Options window, on the Sky page, there is a checkbox for Twinkling. Unchecking this box will turn off twinkling for photosensitive users.
